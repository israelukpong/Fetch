FAQ

Before you ask

Do I need a physical SIM card?

No. Every number is fully virtual and lives in your Fetch dashboard. There's nothing to insert and nothing to lose.

Will it work for WhatsApp and Telegram verification?

Yes — each listing shows exactly which apps that number is confirmed to work with, so you know before you buy.

How long does delivery actually take?

Most numbers are provisioned automatically and appear in your dashboard in under a minute. A small number of premium listings take up to an hour.

Can I keep the same number long-term?

Yes. Renew monthly to keep it, or let it lapse after a one-time verification — your choice, no contract.

What happens if a number stops working?

Support swaps it for a working one at no extra cost. That guarantee applies to every number on the platform.
